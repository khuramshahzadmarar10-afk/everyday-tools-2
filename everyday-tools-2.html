Frequently Asked Questions About Our Free Online Tools

Quick answers about the unit converter, age calculator, color picker, and JSON formatter.

Are these tools free to use?

Yes. All four tools can be used without creating an account or paying a fee.

What information leaves my browser?

Your unit values, birth date, colors, and JSON inputs stay in your browser and are not sent to us. Google Analytics does receive usage data about page visits and interactions.

How does the age calculator handle leap years?

It compares full calendar dates rather than dividing the day count by 365, so month length and leap-year differences are included.

What JSON can the formatter validate?

It accepts valid standard JSON: objects, arrays, strings, numbers, booleans, and null. Comments, trailing commas, and single-quoted keys are not valid JSON.

Privacy Policy

How Everyday Tools 2 handles tool inputs, site analytics, and cookies.

Tool inputs stay in your browser

The values you enter into the Unit Converter, Age Calculator, Color Picker, and JSON Formatter are processed 100% inside your browser. They are not uploaded to us or stored on a server by Everyday Tools 2.

This includes measurements, dates of birth, color codes, and JSON text. Closing or refreshing the page clears any unsaved tool input from the current page session.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use this website. Google Analytics may send site-usage data to Google, such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from an IP address, device and browser information, session activity, and interactions with the site.

That analytics data helps us improve performance and decide which utilities are most useful. Google handles this information under its own privacy terms and data practices.

Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies to distinguish visits, measure usage, and produce aggregated reports. Your browser settings may let you block or delete cookies. Blocking analytics cookies should not prevent the four tools from working because their calculations run locally in your browser.

Third-party links

This website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those services, not by Everyday Tools 2.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through GitHub.

In short: your tool inputs stay local; Google Analytics receives website usage data.
